Marcene Marcus, LCSW

Conceiving Victory, Inc. is owned and operated by Marcene Marcus, LCSW: psychotherapist, licensed clinical social worker, and clinical supervisor in California.

She trained at Columbia University, USC’s Leonard Davis School, and the University of California, Santa Barbara. Her work pairs warmth and humor with evidence-based tools. Specialties include anxiety, depression, grief and loss, maternal mental health, and infertility.

We meet you where you are, from the comfort of your home.

04

The work

A practice for children, adults, couples, and families in hard seasons. Also a place for psychotherapists to consult and receive clinical supervision.

  • For clients Online therapy for individuals, couples, and families.
  • For therapists Online clinical consultation and clinical supervision.
  • For groups Workshops-to-go: you bring the group, date, and topic.

Workshop topics

  • Conflict resolution
  • Getting unstuck
  • Nurturing healthy connections
  • Communication
  • Stress management
  • Time management
  • Boundary setting
  • Assertiveness
  • Other life skills: budgeting, credit, healthy lifestyle

Custom topics welcome. Name what your group needs when you inquire.
